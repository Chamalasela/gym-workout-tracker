Use as an app on your iPhone

Keep using this website, or add it to your Home Screen for an app window of its own.

Move your data first: Safari and a Home Screen app can have separate storage. If the installed app looks empty, your original workouts are still in Safari.

  1. In Safari, use Download dated backup below and save the JSON file to Files. Note the workout, set, and draft counts shown with the backup.
  2. In Safari’s Share menu (sometimes inside More), choose Add to Home Screen. Turn on Open as Web App if offered, then tap Add.
  3. Open Gym tracker from its new icon while online. Wait for Offline ready.
  4. If your workouts are missing, open Data → Choose backup file, select the saved file, and check the preview counts before tapping Restore backup. Restore replaces the data in this app.
  5. Check History and the backup counts in the installed app. Keep your JSON file and the Safari copy until you have verified everything.

After setup, reopen and log workouts without a connection. Your devices and Safari do not automatically sync. Clearing website data can remove workouts and offline access, so keep dated backups.
